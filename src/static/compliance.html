Compliance & Certifications

Saurellius is committed to maintaining the highest standards of regulatory compliance and industry certifications.

Regulatory Compliance

GDPR

General Data Protection Regulation (EU) - We comply with all GDPR requirements for EU users, including data subject rights and lawful processing.

CCPA

California Consumer Privacy Act - We provide California residents with transparency and control over their personal information as required by law.

HIPAA

Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act - While not a healthcare provider, we maintain HIPAA-like security standards for sensitive data.

PCI DSS

Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard - Payment processing is handled by PCI DSS Level 1 compliant providers. We do not store card data.

Industry Certifications

SOC 2 Type II

System and Organization Controls - Our infrastructure undergoes regular SOC 2 Type II audits verifying security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, and privacy.

ISO 27001

Information Security Management - We maintain ISO 27001 standards for information security management systems and controls.

AWS Compliance

Amazon Web Services - Our infrastructure is hosted on AWS, which maintains compliance with SOC 2, ISO 27001, HIPAA, PCI DSS, and other standards.

Data Protection & Privacy

Data Residency

User data is stored in AWS data centers within the United States. EU users' data is processed in compliance with GDPR requirements including Standard Contractual Clauses.

Data Retention

We retain user data as long as the account is active. Upon account deletion, data is securely purged within 30 days. Paystub records may be retained for 7 years for tax purposes if required by law.

Data Breach Notification

In the event of a data breach affecting personal information, we will notify affected users within 72 hours as required by GDPR and other applicable laws.

Tax Compliance

Tax Calculation Accuracy

Our paystub generator uses current tax tables for all 50 U.S. states and territories, updated annually to reflect changes in federal, state, and local tax rates. Tax calculations are based on IRS guidelines and state-specific requirements.

Tax Withholding

We support all standard tax withholding scenarios including federal, FICA (Social Security and Medicare), state income tax, local income tax, and state disability insurance.

Payroll Tax Reporting

While Saurellius generates paystubs, users are responsible for filing required tax forms (W-2, 1099, etc.) with appropriate tax authorities.

Employment Law Compliance

Minimum Wage Compliance

Our system supports federal minimum wage ($7.25/hour) and state-specific minimum wages, which are updated as laws change.

Overtime Calculations

We support overtime calculations including federal overtime (1.5x for hours over 40/week) and state-specific overtime rules.

Deduction Rules

Our system enforces compliance with FLSA (Fair Labor Standards Act) rules regarding legal deductions from paystubs.

Document Security & Authenticity

Bank-Grade Security Features

Generated paystubs include:

  • QR code verification for authenticity
  • Document hashing for integrity verification
  • Microtext security features
  • Watermarks and security patterns
  • Snappt AI compliance verification

PDF Encryption

All generated paystubs are delivered as encrypted PDFs with optional password protection.

Audit & Monitoring

Regular Audits

We conduct quarterly security audits and annual compliance audits by third-party firms to ensure ongoing compliance with all applicable standards.

Continuous Monitoring

Our infrastructure is monitored 24/7 for security threats, compliance violations, and system anomalies using advanced monitoring tools.

Compliance Reporting

We maintain detailed compliance documentation and are prepared for regulatory audits and inspections.

We recommend consulting with a tax professional or employment attorney for complex situations.
