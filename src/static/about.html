About Saurellius

Saurellius Cloud Payroll Stub Manager by Dr. Paystub Corp is a modern, cloud-based platform designed to simplify paystub generation for individuals, freelancers, and small businesses.

Our Mission

We believe that everyone should have access to professional, authentic paystubs quickly and easily. Whether you're applying for an apartment, securing a loan, or managing employee payroll, Saurellius makes it simple to generate bank-grade paystubs that meet professional standards.

Why Saurellius?

Accuracy

100% accurate tax calculations for all 50 states and territories, updated annually with current tax rates.

Security

Military-grade encryption, bank-grade security features, and compliance with GDPR, CCPA, and SOC 2 standards.

Speed

Generate professional paystubs in seconds. Download as PDF instantly for immediate use.

YTD Tracking

Intelligent year-to-date tracking that automatically continues from your last paystub.

Support

Dedicated customer support team available to help with questions and issues.

Rewards

Our Team

Saurellius is built by a team of payroll experts, software engineers, and security professionals dedicated to making paystub generation simple, secure, and accessible to everyone.

Technology Stack

Built on modern, scalable cloud infrastructure with:

  • Python Flask backend with PostgreSQL database
  • AWS Elastic Beanstalk for reliable hosting
  • WeasyPrint for bank-grade PDF generation
  • Stripe for secure payment processing
  • Modern, responsive frontend with Apple-inspired design
